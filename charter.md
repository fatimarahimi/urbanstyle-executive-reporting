## Agreements
1. **How we help each other:** If someone gets stuck, they must first search on Google or ask an AI. If the issue is not resolved within 15 minutes, they share the error message and screenshot in Google Chat without credentials.
2. **Meeting Time:** We value our rest; therefore, we will not schedule meetings outside of the regular programme sessions. We aim to complete all collaborative work during the live hours.
3. **File Sharing:** GitHub serves as our source of truth for code and markdown reports. We use Google Drive to share images, screenshots, and presentation slides.
